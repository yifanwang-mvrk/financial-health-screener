# Sample Design

Last updated: 2026-08-05

## Census and Release Sample

The A1 census contains 26 US-listed or historically US-listed companies spanning inventory-led retail, marketplaces, hybrid commerce, travel, local commerce, and boundary businesses. The reset inherited and froze this 26-company census before the analytical build because it already covered the required operating-model and boundary cases; it does not claim the framework's exploratory 30-40-company upper target or complete industry coverage.

The frozen Q1 release contains six companies with three manually verified fiscal years each:

| Analysis peer group | Companies |
| --- | --- |
| Inventory-led E-commerce | AMZN, CHWY |
| Marketplace / Platform | BKNG, DASH, EBAY, ETSY |

The broad peer labels support descriptive comparison only. Issuer-specific revenue recognition, fiscal calendars, acquisitions, and equity structure remain visible through comparability notes and quality flags.

## Inclusion Rules

- US exchange listing or relevant historical US listing.
- Online commerce or transaction platform is a meaningful business engine.
- Multi-year annual facts are available and can be mapped to the required concepts.
- Business model and revenue-recognition differences can be described rather than hidden.
- Q1 release inclusion requires complete FY2021-FY2023 manually verified financial statements.

## Event Census

Events are stored separately from companies. Each candidate retains the first-public date, date basis, effective date where relevant, SEC source, confidence, theoretical pre-event coverage, verified coverage, eligibility, and exclusion reason.

The five current candidates are an upper-bound census. The framework permits an early stop when point-in-time quarterly and cash-flow reconstruction costs are unacceptable. None of the first five has a verified point-in-time quarterly panel, so further event collection would not rescue the current release and Gate 2 is Tier C / No-Go.

## Frozen Boundary

The census does not automatically expand the analytical sample. The six-company Q1 release remains frozen so that evidence upgrades do not change conclusions after the fact. A future expansion must define a new source cutoff, sample version, and gate decision.
